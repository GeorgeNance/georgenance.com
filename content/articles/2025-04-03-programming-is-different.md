Programming is different now. People who truely love coding, the ones who love the syntax, elegance, and craftsmanship, are seeing a sift in what it means to be a developer. With AI programming on the rise, programmers are focusing lesson the craft and more on managing. Employers are pushing developers to use AI tools to increase producitity.
I am not a hater of AI. I am no luddiete. I have used AI alot to help me coding. I am just noticing how much coding is changing.
